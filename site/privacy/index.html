Privacy, kept local.

TickLens is designed to observe multiplayer traffic shape without collecting the traffic itself.

Library and reports

The npm library does not send telemetry or make network requests. Measurements live in your server process until you clear or export them. Reports contain timing, counts, estimated bytes, room size, and redacted room labels by default. TickLens never captures payload bodies.

Documentation site

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party scripts. The optional Field Kit stores your license token, a daily verification result, and reports you explicitly import in your browser’s local storage. That data does not leave your device except that the license token is sent to the Sociobot verification endpoint.

Purchases

Sociobot and Dodo act as merchant of record and process checkout. Their systems receive the information needed to complete your purchase. TickLens does not receive payment card details.

Your controls

You can remove TickLens site data at any time through your browser’s storage controls. Clearing it removes the local license and saved comparisons; you can restore a valid purchase by pasting the license again.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
