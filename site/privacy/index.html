How TickLens handles data

TickLens measures multiplayer update shape without collecting the update contents.

Library and reports

The npm library does not send telemetry or make network requests. Measurements remain in your server process until you clear or export them. Reports contain timing, counts, estimated bytes, room size, and redacted room labels by default. TickLens does not capture payload bodies.

Documentation site

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party scripts. The optional Field Kit stores a license token, a daily verification result, and reports you import in browser local storage. This information stays on your device except when the license token is sent to Sociobot for verification.

Purchases

Sociobot and Dodo act as merchant of record and process checkout. TickLens does not receive payment card details.

Your controls

You can remove TickLens site data through your browser storage controls. Clearing it removes the local license and saved comparisons. You can restore a valid purchase by pasting the license again.

Contact

Send privacy questions to privacy@sociobot.in.
